# MOBICOM-MCO
The application, Bayanihan Spots, maps and documents small but valuable public micro-spaces across Manila — barangay covered courts, pocket plazas, churchyards, rooftop community gardens, corner study cafés, water refill stations, and weekend pop-ups. Users can discover places by use-case (study, rest, play, market), filter and route to nearby micro-spots, check simple crowd/safety indicators, upload photos and short reviews, save favorites for offline use, propose small community improvements (e.g., plant boxes, trash bins, add shade), and join local volunteer events. The app emphasizes community-sourced information and local relevance for Manila’s dense, multi-use public spaces.
